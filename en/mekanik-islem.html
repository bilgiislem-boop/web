About the Factory

Our cutting department has double-head saws, profile slicing machines, etc. and angled cutting can be done on the relevant machines. We have 3, 3+1, 4+1 and 5 axis CNC machines. We have C type 25, 35, 40, 60 and 80 Ton eccentric presses.
