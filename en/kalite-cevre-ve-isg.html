As ALTİM ALÜMİNYUM, our Quality, Environment, OHS and Energy policies are;

- Our company, which operates in the production of Aluminum Profiles, does not harm the ecological balance with all its activities,
‍
- Ensuring that waste is minimized at the source, reused and recycled when possible and that unused waste is disposed of with appropriate methods,
‍
- Ensuring the efficient use of energy, raw materials and natural resources,
‍
- Preventing any negative effects that may occur on the health of our employees and people who may be affected by our activities; To provide all necessary protection to control accidents, damages and dangers that may occur to our own and other persons' property and properties at the initial stage,
‍
- To develop the Quality, Environment, Occupational Health and Safety and Energy awareness of our employees through the trainings to be provided, and to make the Integrated Management System understanding a philosophy of life,
‍
- To approach occupational health and safety-related dangers with sensitivity and to make the necessary effort to minimize them,
‍
- To work to develop the Quality, Environment and OHS awareness and Energy sensitivity of the relevant parties (Employees, Suppliers, Customers),
‍
- To implement, develop and continuously improve the effectiveness of Quality Management Systems in all processes,
‍
Focusing on customer and party expectations and increasing customer satisfaction,
‍
- To encourage and support team spirit and teamwork with a management approach based on continuous improvement,
‍
- To follow technological innovations in the sector simultaneously with the world, and to ensure that the management Our staff, with our trained and experienced personnel, is to meet the needs and expectations of our customers in accordance with the management system conditions and our goals,
‍
- To prepare the appropriate ground and environment for the implementation and continuity of these policies.
