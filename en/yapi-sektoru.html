Facade Systems

Our facade systems offer aesthetics and durability together on the exteriors of buildings. These profiles adapt to modern architectural designs, increase the energy efficiency of the structures and provide high protection against external factors.

Glass Balcony / Winter Garden

Our glass balcony and winter garden profiles are designed to add extra comfort and elegance to your living spaces.

Joinery Profiles

Our joinery profiles provide excellent sealing and thermal insulation in door and window systems. It adapts to your projects with different design and color options and increases the energy efficiency of the structures.

Dilatasyon Profiles

Dilatasyon profiles are profiles that cover the dilatation separations created to prevent fractures caused by movements and stresses in wide and large structures, aesthetically and functionally.

Sun Shading Profile

Our sunshade profiles provide energy savings by ensuring that buildings benefit from sunlight at an optimum level. They adapt to modern architecture with their aesthetic and functional designs.

Cable Channel

Our cable channel profiles ensure that cables are placed regularly and safely. It is suitable for both indoor and outdoor applications with its aesthetic and functional design.

Skirting Profile

Our skirting profiles provide an aesthetic and durable finish in indoor spaces. They adapt to your decoration with different color and design options.

Shower Cabin Profile

Our shower cabin profiles are designed to add elegance and durability to your bathrooms. It offers long-lasting use with its water-resistant structure and aesthetic appearance.

Threshold Profile

Our threshold profiles are decorative and functional profiles used in door thresholds and transitions of different floor coverings.

Tile Lath Profile

Tile lath profiles provide smooth and aesthetic tile flooring provides an end and transition.

Partition Wall Profile

Our partition wall profiles help you create flexible and functional areas in interior spaces. It offers easy installation with its lightweight and durable structure.

Handrail Profile

Our handrail profiles offer safety and aesthetics together on stairs and balconies. It adds value to your projects with its durable structure and stylish design.

Clean Room Profiles

Our clean room profiles are perfect solution products for areas requiring hygiene and sterilization. It draws attention with its easy-to-clean structure.

Parapet Profile

These are profiles used for security and aesthetic purposes in balconies and terraces.
