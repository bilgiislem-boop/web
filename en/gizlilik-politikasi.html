Credit Card Security

The security of your credit card is provided by SSL encryption technology. You will see a lock icon at the bottom of your browser on every page where you enter your credit card information. This icon represents a 128-Bit SSL Certificate. In addition, the internet address section will change from http to https on the pages where you enter your credit card information while making a payment. With SSL technology, when the payment process starts, a direct encrypted communication is established between the user computer and the bank, regardless of the site, and no information you send through your browser is seen by https://altim.com.tr/ employees or third parties. When you click on the icon that appears as Secure Payment on our site, you can access the records related to our security certificate.
