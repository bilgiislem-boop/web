Surface Treatment Factory

There is an anodizing coating facility and an electrostatic powder painting unit in the surface treatment factory area.

Aluminum profiles arriving as raw material are shipped to the appropriate production unit according to the customer's request.

There are a total of 28 pools in our anodizing facility. The chemical values ​​of the pools are analyzed in the laboratory. Necessary chemical additions are made according to the analysis results.

Mechanical Processing Factory

Altim Aluminum's aluminum mechanical processing factory produces solutions to the needs of the sector by offering products designed with high precision. With our advanced technological infrastructure, we carry out many processes such as customer-specific cutting, drilling and bending.
